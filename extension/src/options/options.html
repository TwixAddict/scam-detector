Scam Detector settings

Where to check

Scam Detector isn't allowed to read Gmail and Outlook yet, so it can't put warnings above your emails automatically.

Language

How strict

Trusted senders

For these domains the wording of the message isn't flagged. Links and attachments are still checked. One domain per line.

Your organization's domains

Used to catch outsiders pretending to be a colleague (e.g. schooI.be with a capital I instead of an l). Your own Gmail domain is added automatically if it isn't a free mail address.

Privacy

Emails are analyzed on this device. Nothing about your mail is sent anywhere.

About

Scam Detector is a school project. It is open source: anyone can read, check and improve the code.
