A school project, open source. Scam Detector was made as a school project. All of its code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read exactly what it does, check it, or improve it. It is not made by, or connected to, Google, Microsoft or any company it mentions.

No checker catches every scam. Treat a green card as "nothing obvious found", not as a guarantee.

How it works

1

Open an email

In Gmail or Outlook on the web. There's nothing to click: the extension reads the email that is open on your screen.

2

It's checked on your device

More than 70 warning signs: fake senders, lookalike links, dangerous attachments, pressure tactics and more. Your mail never leaves your computer.

3

You get a clear card

Red for a likely scam, orange for suspicious, green when nothing was found, with the reasons in plain language.

What it looks for

Sender

  • Names like "PayPal" sent from an address that isn't PayPal's
  • Lookalike domains such as paypa1.com or rnicrosoft.com
  • Random-looking addresses and failed sender checks (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

Links

  • Links that show one address but open another
  • Known phishing links (public OpenPhish list, checked locally)
  • Shortened links, free hosting, "contact us on WhatsApp"

Attachments

  • Programs (.exe, .js, .iso…) and web pages sent as files
  • Double extensions like invoice.pdf.exe
  • Password-protected archives with the password in the mail

Wording and tricks

  • Urgency, requests for passwords or card details, gift cards and crypto
  • Parcel fees, fake invoices, "you've won", blackmail, casino and pirate-TV spam
  • Hidden text and invisible characters meant to fool spam filters

Your privacy

Emails are analyzed inside your browser and are never sent anywhere. The extension collects no data and has no analytics. The only download is the public OpenPhish list of phishing links, which is compared with your links on your own device. Read the privacy policy.
