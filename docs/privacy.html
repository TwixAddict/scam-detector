Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Applies to the Scam Detector extension for Firefox and for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera), version 1.0.0 and later.

Short version: Scam Detector collects no data. Your emails are checked inside your own browser and are never sent to anyone, including the makers of the extension. Scam Detector is a school project, and its full source code is public on GitHub, so you can verify everything on this page.

What the extension reads

  • When you open an email in Gmail (mail.google.com) or Outlook on the web (outlook.live.com, outlook.office.com, outlook.office365.com), the extension reads that email in the page: sender, subject, text, links, attachment names and images.
  • When you click Scan this page in the popup, it reads the page you are on, once.
  • When you paste an email into Check email source, it reads what you pasted.

All of this is analyzed on your device. Nothing you read or paste is uploaded, logged or shared.

What is stored, and where

Only in your browser's extension storage, on your computer:

  • Your settings (language, sensitivity, trusted domains, and so on).
  • Remembered senders: the addresses and names of people who emailed you, used to spot impostors who copy a known name. You can turn this off and erase it in Settings at any time.
  • The phishing link list (see below).
  • Temporary results for the tab you have open, so the toolbar button can show them. These are deleted when the tab closes.

Network connections

The extension makes one kind of request: every 6 hours it downloads the public OpenPhish list of known phishing links (from openphish.com, which serves it from GitHub). It is a plain download of a public file. No information about you or your emails is sent with it. Your links are compared with the list on your own computer. You can turn this off in Settings.

There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no accounts.

Permissions, and why

  • Access to Gmail and Outlook on the web: to read the open email and show the warning card above it.
  • Access to openphish.com: to download the phishing link list.
  • storage: to keep your settings and the items listed above on your computer.
  • alarms: to refresh the phishing list every 6 hours.
  • activeTab and scripting: only used when you click Scan this page, to check the page you are looking at.

Children

The extension collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's history on GitHub. Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/TwixAddict/scam-detector/issues.
